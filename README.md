# Complaint Management Portal

A web-based **Complaint Management Portal** designed to provide a centralized platform for registering, tracking, managing, and resolving complaints digitally.

The system helps users submit complaints online and allows administrators and department staff to manage complaints efficiently through a structured workflow.

---

##  Project Overview

The Complaint Management Portal replaces traditional manual complaint-handling processes with a centralized digital system.

Users can submit complaints, track their progress, receive updates, and provide feedback after resolution. Administrators can manage users, departments, complaints, staff assignments, and reports.

---

##  Objectives

- Provide an online platform for complaint registration.
- Allow users to track complaint status.
- Assign complaints to appropriate departments and staff.
- Maintain complete complaint history.
- Improve transparency in complaint handling.
- Provide notifications for complaint updates.
- Allow users to provide feedback after resolution.
- Generate complaint-related reports and statistics.

---

##  User Roles

###  User / Complainant

- Register and login
- Submit complaints
- View complaint details
- Track complaint status
- View complaint history
- Receive notifications
- Provide feedback

###  Staff

- Login to the portal
- View assigned complaints
- Review complaint details
- Update complaint status
- Add remarks
- Communicate with users
- Resolve complaints

###  Administrator

- Manage users
- Manage staff
- Manage departments
- View all complaints
- Assign complaints
- Monitor complaint progress
- Handle escalated complaints
- Generate reports
